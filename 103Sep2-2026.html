You can now build on UXP in every Creative Cloud flagship app, with After Effects and Illustrator joining Photoshop, InDesign, Premiere, and Media Encoder over the next two years. From December 2029, CEP will no longer be included in new versions of these apps. In March 2027, the Adobe Marketplace will stop accepting new CEP plugin submissions for Photoshop. UXP uses a different security and architectural model, so most migrations are not a 1:1 port. Read the blog for milestones app by app, migration guides, and how hybrid plugins can handle harder use cases.

The UXP team is taking migration questions in the developer forums.
