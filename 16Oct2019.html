  Premiere Pro: New World scripting progress report  
 

As you've likely heard, Premiere Pro and Adobe Media Encoder (AME) are updating their internal scripting implementation. Specifically, we had planned to default to "New World" scripting (calling your existing ExtendScript code, through our new plumbing), as of our MAX releases.

Thanks to testing by our developers, we've found several issues. Because we won't have all of them fixed in time for MAX, we are not going to change the default behavior; ExtendScript will continue to be executed in "Old World" [the way it's always been called].

We do plan to have all known New World issues fixed, in releases before NAB, and will change our applications' default behavior once that happens. We'll keep you informed.

Thanks for creating integrations with our products!

Bruce Bullis | Senior Engineering Manager | Pro Video Integration and Film

ExtendScript ToolKit and macOS 10.15 Catalina

If you absolutely depend upon the capabilities of ESTK, you should not update to macOS 10.15 Catalina at this time. Read more on the Adobe Tech Blog.

If you wish to upgrade, you can use the VSCode ExtendScript Debugger plugin. This does not replace all the capabilities of ESTK, so you’ll want to be sure that it will meet your needs before you upgrade to Catalina.
